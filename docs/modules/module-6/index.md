## 🎯 Module Overview (Preview)

Module 6 explores **AI-enhanced GIS workflows**, teaching you to integrate artificial intelligence into spatial analysis while maintaining professional judgment. You'll learn to:

- Deploy and interact with local LLMs (Ollama) in containerized environments
- Use Model Context Protocol (MCP) for GIS-specific AI interactions
- Apply prompt engineering techniques for spatial analysis tasks
- Critically evaluate ethical implications of AI in GIS
- Integrate AI tools into professional GIS workflows
- Assess when AI enhancement adds value vs. introduces risk

**Why This Matters:** AI is rapidly transforming GIS practice, from automated feature extraction to intelligent data analysis. Understanding how to ethically and effectively integrate AI tools distinguishes forward-thinking GIS professionals and opens new career possibilities in spatial data science.
